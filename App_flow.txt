# SKOLA — APP FLOW

**Status:** FINAL · LOCKED · IMPLEMENTATION‑READY

---

## 0. ENTRY POINT (ALL USERS)

**Flow**

Login → Role Detection → Active Role Selection → Redirect

A user may have multiple roles (e.g. Teacher + HOD + Parent).

After login, the user selects:
- Active School
- Active Role
- Active Section (if school is bilingual)

The system dynamically loads permissions based on the selected context.

---

## 1. SCHOOL REGISTRATION & INITIAL SETUP (ONE‑TIME)

**Actor:** School Owner / Initial Admin

### Flow

1. Register school
2. Select school type:
   - English
   - French
   - Bilingual
3. System auto‑creates:
   - School entity
   - Academic Sections (English / French if bilingual)
4. Complete school profile:
   - Address
   - Academic calendar defaults
5. Create initial admin roles:
   - Principal
   - Vice Principal
   - Bursar (optional)

➡️ System transitions to **normal operations mode**

## 6. TEACHER FLOW

### Dashboard
- Assigned classes & subjects
- Today’s timetable
- Pending actions

### Core Actions
- Take attendance
- Log lessons (Digital Cahier de Texte):
  - Confirm planned lesson
  - Mark status:
    - Taught
    - Partially taught
    - Not taught

- Upload assignments
- Comment on report cards
- Sign contract (if pending)

### Restrictions
⚠️ Teaching, grading, and attendance permissions are limited until the contract is signed.
