# SKOLA — FINAL PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

**Status:** FINAL · LOCKED · SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH

---

## 1. PRODUCT DEFINITION

SKOLA is a web‑based school management platform designed to digitally replace the official academic and administrative books used in secondary schools.

It faithfully models real‑world school governance, including multiple administrators, departments, bilingual sections, inspections, and compliance requirements.

SKOLA is architected to scale from a single school to thousands without structural redesign.

---

## 2. TARGET INSTITUTIONS

- Secondary schools only
- English, French, and Bilingual systems
- Public and private institutions

---

## 3. CORE PHILOSOPHY

If a school normally records it in a book, register, or official file, SKOLA must manage it digitally in a way that is:

- Reliable
- Secure
- Auditable
- Inspection‑ready

Convenience must never compromise accountability or realism.

---

## 4. USER ROLES & GOVERNANCE (CRITICAL)

### 4.1 Administrative Roles (Multiple per School)

A school may have multiple administrators, each with scoped authority.

System‑defined standard roles (permission‑based, not title‑based):

- Principal (full oversight)
- Vice Principal (academic & discipline supervision)
- Head of Department (HOD) — per subject
- Class Master — per class
- Discipline Master
- Bursar (fees & payments)
- Academic Supervisor
- General Administrative Staff

⚠️ Titles do not grant power. Permissions do.

---

### 4.2 Academic & Stakeholder Roles

- Teacher (subject‑ and class‑based)
- Student (read‑only academic access)
- Parent (multi‑child, multi‑school access)

---

## 5. BILINGUAL SCHOOL ARCHITECTURE (NON‑NEGOTIABLE)

A school may register as:

- English only
- French only
- Bilingual

### If Bilingual

The system creates two **Academic Sections**:
- English Section
- French Section

Each section has its own:
- Subjects
- HODs
- Classes
- Schemes of work
- Grading structures

They share:
- School identity
- Parent accounts
- Student identities
- Report card authority
- Fees system (section‑configurable if required)

➡️ Academically, the system behaves like two schools.  
➡️ Administratively, it behaves as one institution.

---

## 6. ACADEMIC STRUCTURE

### 6.1 Academic Session
- Year‑based (e.g. 2025/2026)
- One active session per school at a time

### 6.2 Terms & Sequences
- Fully flexible per school
- Supports:
  - 1st, 2nd, 3rd Term
  - Variable number of sequences per term (Cameroon‑style)

### 6.3 Subjects
- Defined per academic section
- Fully customizable per school
- Coefficients supported
- No hard‑coded subject lists

---

## 7. CORE MODULES (OFFICIAL PAPERWORK REPLACEMENT)

### 7.1 Scheme of Work
- Created by HOD
- Per subject, class, and academic session
- Locked after approval
- Serves as the authoritative teaching plan
- Feeds directly into lesson tracking

---

### 7.2 Digital Cahier de Texte (Lesson Tracking)
- Lessons logged against approved schemes of work
- Status options:
  - Taught
  - Partially taught
  - Not taught
- Time‑restricted logging
- Attendance‑linked
- Teaching progress auto‑calculated
- HOD & admin oversight
- Full audit trail (no silent edits)

---

### 7.3 Attendance Registers
- Class attendance (primary)
- Subject attendance (derived)
- Absence thresholds
- Parent visibility
- Integrated with discipline rules

---

### 7.4 Continuous Assessment (CA)
- Marks recorded per sequence
- Automatic totals & averages
- Locked after deadlines
- All changes logged
- Approval required for overrides

---

### 7.5 Discipline Records
- Digital incident logging
- Linked to student profile
- Escalation rules
- Parent read‑only visibility
- Included in inspection reports

---

### 7.6 Report Cards
- Automatic generation
- School‑specific PDF templates
- Teacher comments
- Attendance summaries
- Admin approval required before release
- Permanently archived

---

### 7.7 Teacher Contracts & Staff Files
- Contract created during onboarding
- Digital signing by teacher
- Status:
  - Pending
  - Signed
- Teaching permissions blocked until signed
- Full staff compliance history maintained

---

### 7.8 Fees & Payments
- Fee structure definition
- Student balances
- Parent payments
- Digital receipts
- Financial reports & exports

---

### 7.9 Parent Communication
- Announcements
- Academic warnings
- Discipline notices
- Fee notifications
- Read receipts for accountability

---

### 7.10 Inspection & Supervision
- Teaching coverage reports
- Attendance statistics
- Discipline summaries
- Teacher compliance reports
- Exportable inspection packs (PDF / print‑ready)

---

## 8. PARENT & STUDENT EXPERIENCE

### 8.1 Parent
- One account → multiple children
- Children may belong to:
  - Different classes
  - Different sections
  - Different schools
- Real‑time visibility:
  - Attendance
  - Grades
  - Discipline
  - Fees

---

### 8.2 Student
- Read‑only access to:
  - Timetable
  - Results
  - Attendance status
  - Discipline status

- Async‑ready (PDFs, notifications, exports)
- Strong data isolation per school
